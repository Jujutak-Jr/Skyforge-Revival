About Skyforge Revival

A Dead MMO, Revived by Its Community

Skyforge was a science-fantasy MMORPG in which players took on the role of Immortals, fighting invaders on the planet Aelion and climbing the game's ascension system toward godhood. After the official servers shut down, the game became unreachable for new and returning players alike.

Skyforge Revival is a fan-built server emulator that recreates the original world, classes, and progression so the community can keep playing. The project is run independently and non-commercially.

All content, progress, and timelines are currently in active development — see the status panel and the installation section below for details.

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear the most, answered plainly.

Is Skyforge Revival legal?

Skyforge Revival is a non-commercial, fan-run project built by players who wanted to keep the game alive after the official servers closed. We don't sell anything, and we're not affiliated with the original rightsholders. We do our best to run this responsibly, but as with any private server project, you should decide for yourself whether you're comfortable joining.

Will it cost anything to play?

No. Creating an account and playing on Skyforge Revival is completely free and always will be.

Do I need to own the original game?

Yes — you'll need the original Skyforge client files installed on your PC. We don't host or distribute the game client itself, only the launcher that connects it to our servers.

When will the server launch?

We don't have a fixed date — this is a volunteer project and things take the time they take. Check the Roadmap above for where we currently stand, or join our Discord for the latest updates.

Will my account or characters be wiped later?

During early testing phases, wipes are common as things get rebuilt and improved. We'll clearly announce any wipe before it happens, and let you know once the server is stable for the long term.

How can I help the project?

Join our Discord! We're always happy to have testers, feedback, and community members helping each other out. No technical skills required.
